Coding Services

Three coding systems. Clearly defined applications.

Support the representation of documented diagnoses, medical services, procedures, and applicable supplies through the appropriate coding system.

01 / DIAGNOSES
ICD-10-CM

Diagnosis Coding

Review documented diagnoses and select appropriate codes, considering the specificity supported by the medical record.

  • Documented diagnosis details
  • Supported specificity and laterality
  • Sequencing and coding instructions
Diagnosis coding based on the documented encounter.
02 / SERVICES
CPT

Service & Procedure Coding

Review documented medical services and procedures to support code selection within the agreed specialties and care settings.

  • Documented services and procedures
  • Required supporting details
  • Modifier applicability, where relevant
CPT is HCPCS Level I; scope follows the service setting.
03 / ITEMS & SERVICES
HCPCS LEVEL II

Additional Coding Support

Support code selection for applicable products, supplies, and services not represented by CPT, based on the record.

  • Item or service identification
  • Documented quantities and units
  • Applicable modifiers and details
Applicable items and services within the agreed scope.

Code selection depends on the documentation, care setting, current coding guidance, and applicable payer requirements.
